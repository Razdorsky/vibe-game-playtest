# Recorded SFX Sources

All files in this folder are runtime subsets of permissive OpenGameArt assets.

- `../processed/*.wav`
  - Source: OpenGameArt, "Gunshot Sounds" by Tabasco.
  - URL: https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
  - License: CC0.
  - Runtime use: processed player/enemy muzzle blast and distant firearm tail
    layers. Source recordings and the deterministic processing script are kept
    under `assets/raw/audio/opengameart/gunshot-sounds/` and `scripts/`.
- `foley/*.ogg`
  - Source: OpenGameArt, "100 CC0 SFX" by rubberduck.
  - URL: https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx
  - License: CC0.
  - Runtime use: explosion, glass, body hit/fall, metal casing/magazine, and mechanical weapon layers.
- `helicopter/helicopter.mp3`
  - Source: project-owner supplied `0713.MP3` on 2026-07-13.
  - License: project-provided asset; external provenance was not specified.
  - Raw path: `assets/raw/audio/user/0713.MP3`.
  - Runtime use: mandatory recorded helicopter rotor loop, repeated across its full 2.534-second duration.
